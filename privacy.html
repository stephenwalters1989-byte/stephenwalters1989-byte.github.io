Privacy policy

This policy describes Stephen Walters’s personal installation of Podcast Shorts Factory and this informational website.

Data accessed and its purpose

The local app uses Google OAuth credentials to access the authorised YouTube channel. It uses channel and video identifiers, titles, descriptions, upload and scheduling details, and available performance metrics such as views, likes and retention to upload videos, manage scheduling and evaluate performance. Its granted permissions also allow YouTube content and comment management; automatic comment posting is not currently configured. Optional research features can read public video information and comments.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials, channel records, metrics, transcripts, rendered videos and logs are stored on the operator’s computer. Local records are retained until the operator removes them; there is no automatic deletion period. Uploaded videos and associated metadata are stored by YouTube under its own policies.

Services receiving data

Video uploads and channel API requests are sent to Google/YouTube. AI features use OpenRouter and its selected model providers. Prompts can include transcripts, video titles and descriptions, selected video frames, performance summaries and public comments used for analysis. Web research sends search queries to search providers. These services process requests under their respective policies. OAuth tokens and API keys are used to authenticate their respective services and are not intended to be included in AI prompts or published on this website.

Google user data is used for the features described here, not sold or used for advertising. Podcast Shorts Factory’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

The channel owner can revoke access through Google Account connections and remove locally stored credentials and records. Revoking access stops future authorised requests; it does not itself delete existing local data, service-provider records or videos already uploaded to YouTube. YouTube content can be managed separately in YouTube Studio.

Website visitors

This site does not use analytics scripts, advertising cookies or forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard technical request data under GitHub’s privacy statement.
